About Rock Hard

What it is: a songwriting app for Robert. Type a few lyrics, or describe a song in plain words, and it writes the whole song — verse, chorus, bridge, outro — then plays a backing track in the genre so you can sing or rap over it.

Honest disclosure: the lyrics are written by a rule-based engine running on your phone. It is not a real AI model and it does not sing. There is no AI vocalist in this app — real AI singing needs a paid service, and this app is $0 forever.

How the songs are built: the engine keeps your lines word for word, reads your description for the subject, the people, the emotions, the message, and the story direction, then writes each section with a job to do — verse 1 introduces the situation, verse 2 develops the story, the chorus says the central message, the bridge turns the story, and the outro resolves it. It checks its own work for repeated lines, broken rhymes, lines that are hard to perform, and lines that do not read as proper sentences.

Offline: once the app has loaded once, it opens again with no connection. Your songs live on this phone, and the app itself is cached on this phone too.

Your data: everything lives on this phone in local storage. No accounts, no servers, no tracking, no ads. Export a backup from the Songbook tab so a lost phone never means lost songs.

Put it on your home screen: on Android, open this page in Chrome, tap the three dots, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full-screen like a real app. On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
